## Description (Rich Text)

**Run your entire fleet operation from one dashboard — for less than a month of enterprise software.**

RZ Dispatch is a complete, self-hosted fleet operations platform. Dispatch jobs to drivers, track vehicles on a live map, take bookings and quotes online, manage your customer relationships, and give your drivers and customers their own portals — all with a polished, modern interface and no monthly fees.

Companies like yours pay $30–$50 per vehicle per month for dispatch software. With RZ Dispatch you pay **once** and own it forever.

**WHAT'S INCLUDED**

- **Live dispatch queue** — every job, priority, driver and ETA on one screen
- **Interactive map view** — see your whole fleet in real time
- **Driver assignment** — assign or reassign drivers in one click
- **Daily schedule board** — flip through days, every day shows its own jobs
- **Booking & quote engine** — customers request a ride, get an instant estimate
- **Vehicle tiers** — price by car, SUV, van, or luxury automatically
- **Customer CRM** — every customer, their history, and their spend
- **Customer portal** — self-service quote and booking lookup

- **Stripe licensing included** — the purchase/license flow is already built and tested
