This policy explains what data RZ Dispatch collects, why, and the choices you have.

2. How data is used

  • To operate the platform: authentication, dispatch, assignment, tracking, and messaging.
  • To send notifications you opted into (email, in-app, and SMS where configured).
  • To secure the service: rate limiting, account lockout, and audit logging.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Notification choices

You can control email, SMS, and in-app notifications in your account preferences. Disabling a channel stops operational messages sent through it.

4. Third parties

  • Stripe — payment processing. RZ Dispatch does not store card numbers.
  • SMTP provider — email delivery.
  • Mapping / geocoding — used only when you request a distance estimate; the address you enter is sent to the configured provider.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to run the platform, or where required by law.

6. Retention

Operational records are retained for as long as your organization keeps the account active. You may request deletion of account data by contacting your administrator or support.

7. Security

We use access control, password hashing, session management, rate limiting, audit logging, and encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns to support.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact support to exercise these rights.

9. Cookies

RZ Dispatch uses a session cookie to keep signed-in users authenticated, plus local storage for preferences and tokens on your own device.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
